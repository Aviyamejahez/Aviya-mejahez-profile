About me

Hi! I’m Aviya, 33, from Rosh HaAyin 🇮🇱.

I’m a person who lives for experiences—whether it’s traveling the world ✈️, dancing 💃, or meeting new people 🤝.

I have a huge heart for animals 🐰 (proud owner of four rabbits!) and I love the art of hosting ☕.

For 12 years, I owned and managed a successful home pastry shop, 'Aviya Patisserie' 🍰.

While baking was a childhood dream fulfilled, the industry is incredibly demanding.

After a transformative six-month chapter in New York 🗽, where I even started a new business venture, I decided it was time to pursue my next big dream: a career in Tech 💻.

I am currently diving into QA and Automation 🔍.

I chose this field because I want to lead, innovate, and eventually have the freedom to work remotely from anywhere in the world 🌍.

I bring the same dedication and 'Sisyphic' hard work from the pastry world into my code.

I’m a firm believer in giving 100% to everything I do—and I know this is just the beginning! ✨
