Aviya in the News- macaronflex

  • June, 2020
  • Mako

In 2020, at the start of the The COVID-19 pandemic, the world came to a halt and celebrations were put on hold. As a result, my work in my bakery dropped significantly. To fill the sudden void, I began experimenting in the kitchen. One of those experiments was "mini macarons", which unexpectedly became a huge hit. What started as a fun way to relieve boredom quickly turned into a sensation after I shared it with the "Mothers Cooking Together" Facebook group. The response was overwhelming. I continued creating and sharing new products, catching the attention of Mako, who featured my story in an article.
